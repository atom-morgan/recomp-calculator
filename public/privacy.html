Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information skinnyfatcalculator.com (“the site”) collects and how it’s used. The site is run by Cinnabar Calisthenics LLC (“we”, “us”), based in Georgia, USA.

The short version

  • What you type into the calculator never leaves your browser. We don’t see it or store it.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and a few button clicks. It uses cookies, and you can block it.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads or use advertising trackers.

What you enter in the calculator

Your weight, body fat, sex, age group, daily steps and training time are used to work out your numbers right there in your browser. They aren’t sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and they aren’t saved. They’re gone as soon as you close or reload the page.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people use the site and how they find it. It collects:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay
  • how you arrived, such as the site or social media post that linked to us
  • your approximate location (city and country), worked out from your IP address
  • your device type, browser, operating system and screen size
  • a few clicks: getting your results, switching between goal tabs (and which goal), and clicking “Apply for coaching” or our Instagram link

Google Analytics does not receive anything you enter into the calculator. It sets cookies (_ga and _ga_*) that recognise your browser on return visits, which last up to two years. Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Detailed event data is kept for two months, and summary reports are kept longer. We haven’t turned on Google’s advertising features.

You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. To opt out, you can block cookies in your browser, use a tracking blocker, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The calculator works the same either way.

Other services the site uses

  • Netlify hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and basic request details in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s typeface, so your browser requests it from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Coaching applications. “Apply for coaching” opens a Google Form. Anything you submit there comes to us and is used only to review your application and contact you about coaching. Google’s privacy policy covers how Google handles the form itself.
  • Instagram. Our Instagram link takes you to Instagram, where Instagram’s own privacy policy applies.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent personal information, share it with advertisers, run ad retargeting, or require an account.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, such as the EU, the UK or certain US states, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. We don’t hold your calculator entries, and analytics data isn’t linked to your name, so usually there’s nothing we can tie to you. If you’ve applied for coaching, we can delete your application on request. Just email us.

Children

The site isn’t intended for children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
