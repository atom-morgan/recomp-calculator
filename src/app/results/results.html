What is TDEE?

Your TDEE is how many calories you burn in a normal day. It's built from your resting burn (what keeps you alive), your daily movement (walking, chores, fidgeting), your training, and a small amount from digesting food.

Most people overestimate their training and underestimate their movement. That's why steps matter as much as workouts, and why the number above is a starting point, not a verdict. Eat at these targets for two to three weeks, watch your weight and waist, and adjust from there.
