How your targets are set

Each target starts from your maintenance calories and moves up or down from there.

  • Standard recomp: about {{ recompPct }}% below maintenance. A small deficit, so fat comes down while hard training keeps building muscle.
  • Fat loss focus: about {{ fatPct }}% below maintenance{{ fatSeniorNote }}. Faster fat loss, with higher protein to protect your muscle.
  • Muscle focus: about {{ musclePct }}% above maintenance. A slight surplus to support strength and size, with slower fat loss.

Protein is based on your lean mass, not your total weight, and we never suggest fewer than {{ floor.male | number }} calories a day for men or {{ floor.female | number }} for women.
